# Sledders-Map-Generator
A free map generator for the game Sledders. Use real life locations and make your own Sledders  maps.
## How to Create a Sledders Map

1. **Download and install** – Download the installer for your operating system, install Sledders Map Generator and launch the application.
2. **Create a new map** – Start a new map project and enter your map name.
3. **Choose a location** – Search for a city, region or location on the map and select your desired area.
4. **Choose map size** – Select the game map size you want.
5. **Download data** – Download the terrain and forest data for your selected area.
6. **Generate the map** – Continue to the Generate tab and generate the Sledders map files.
7. **Export to Sledders** – Export the generated files to your Sledders Custom Maps folder.
8. **Test your map** – Launch Sledders, open the Level Editor and find your map under the Create Content tab.
9. **Adjust your map** – Test the terrain and adjust tree density or other settings in Sledders Map Generator if needed. Regenerate and export again until you are happy with the result.
10. **Add your own icon** – Replace the default map icon with your own custom image.
11. **Package for mod.io** – Create a ZIP package containing your finished map files.
12. **Upload to mod.io** – Create a [mod.io](https://mod.io/) account, visit the [Sledders mod.io page](https://sledders.mod.io/), and upload your ZIP file. Add a title, description, screenshots and other requested information.
13. **Publish** – Submit your map and wait for processing. Once published, it should appear in the Sledders Mod Hub in-game.

**Tip:** Test your map thoroughly in Sledders before uploading it to mod.io.
